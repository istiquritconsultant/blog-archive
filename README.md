# Blog Archive — Istiqur IT Consultant

This repository collects original writing from **[Istiqur IT Consultant](https://istiquritconsultant.com/)** — a personal tech diary written by **Md. Istiqur Rahman**, covering AI, Windows, Linux, workflow optimization, and self-hosting notes from real, hands-on work rather than secondhand advice.

## What this blog actually is

Istiqur IT Consultant is not a marketing blog dressed up as a tech diary. It is the ongoing notebook of someone who has spent 18+ years in the field — a career that started, unusually, as a pharmacist before a slow, self-taught pull toward computers turned into a full career change. That background matters here: nothing on this blog is theory borrowed from a course or repackaged from someone else's post. Every entry is a record of something that was actually built, actually broken, actually fixed, or actually learned the hard way, then written up honestly, mistakes included.

The blog deliberately avoids being a sales funnel. There is no pitch inside the posts, no affiliate padding, and no content written to hit a publishing calendar rather than to say something real. If a topic isn't worth writing about yet, nothing goes out that week. That discipline is part of why the archive stays small and dense rather than large and diluted.

## What it covers

The archive is organized around six real categories:

- **AI blogs** — practical AI tools, workflows, and experiments tested against real client and personal projects, prompts and patterns that held up past the first try, and the honest failures that didn't survive a real dataset.
- **Workflow optimization blogs** — systems, shortcuts, and automations that actually save real time.
- **Windows blogs** — real day-to-day Windows troubleshooting, configuration, and fixes.
- **Linux blogs** — hands-on daily-driver Linux notes: shell/terminal workflows, real troubleshooting write-ups, small automation scripts, and the Windows–Linux crossover including dual-boot and WSL.
- **Self-hosting blogs** — real services on real hardware or a real VPS, setup mistakes included.
- **Tech journey blogs** — the career-change story in full: pharmacist background, the self-taught turn into tech, and the decisions along the way.
